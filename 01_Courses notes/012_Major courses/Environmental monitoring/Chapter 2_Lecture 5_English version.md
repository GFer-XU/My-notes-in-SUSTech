色谱 Chromatography

色谱又称色层法或层析法，是一种物理化学分析方法，它利用不同溶质（样品）与固定相和流动相之间的作用力（分配、吸附、离子交换等）的差别，当两相做相对移动时，各溶质在两相间进行多次平衡，使各溶质达到相互分离。

Chromatography, also known as chromatography or chromatography, is a physical and chemical analysis method that utilizes the differences in forces (distribution, adsorption, ion exchange, etc.) between different solutes (samples) and the stationary and mobile phases. When the two phases move relative to each other, each solute undergoes multiple equilibrations between the two phases to achieve mutual separation.

# 离子选择电极法：

# Ion selective electrode method

- 氟离子选择电极是一种以氟化镧(LaF₃)单晶片为敏感膜的传感器
- Fluoride ion selective electrode is a sensor with a lanthanum fluoride (LaF3) single wafer as a sensitive film
- 由于单晶结构对能进入晶格交换的离子有严格的限制，所以有良好的选择性。
- Since the single crystal structure has strict limits on the ions that can enter the lattice, it has good selectivity.

对于氟离子选择电极，较佳的试剂酸度条件为pH5~6。

1. LaF3单晶膜； 2. 内参比浴液 pH&#x3C;5时，溶液中会发生下述弱酸配位反应：2F- + H+ = HF + F = HF2，使溶液中的F减少，会影响电极的灵敏度，使分析结果偏低。这是由于氟电极只对F响应对HF或HF2无响应，而且氟电极的氟化镧电极膜会增大被溶解，影响测定。

3. Ag-AgCl（内参比）电极； 4. 电极管

# 氮 Nitrogen

# Measurement

有机氮：使用凯氏定氮法 。先煮沸以去除氨，然后消解，有机氮通过热和酸的作用转化为铵。

Organic nitrogen: Kjeldahl method. First boil the water to remove the ammonia, then digest to convert the organic nitrogen to ammonium through the action of heat and acid.

总凯氏氮：与有机氮相同，只是不去除氨。

Total Kjeldahl nitrogen (TKN): same as organic nitrogen except that the ammonia is not driven off.

氨：提高pH值，用蒸汽蒸馏出氨，冷凝蒸汽，比色测定，滴定测量，或用特定离子电极测量。

Ammonia: raising pH, distilling off the ammonia with the steam, condensing the steam, determined with colorimetry, measured with titration or with specific-ion electrodes.

亚硝酸盐：比色测定，不稳定，易氧化成硝酸盐。

Nitrite: determined with colorimetry, unstable and easily oxidized to the nitrate form.

硝酸盐：氧化最多的氮，用比色法测定，或用特定离子电极测定。

Nitrate: the most oxidized nitrogen, determined with colorimetry, or with specific-ion electrodes.

# Example

计算1.67 x 10⁻³mol葡萄糖（C₆H₁₂O₆）的理论需氧量：

C₆H₁₂O₆ + O₂ 🡪 CO₂ + H₂O

C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂ 🡪 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O

1.67x 10⁻³mol glucose/L ⨯ 6 mol O₂/ mol glucose ⨯ 32 g O₂/mol O₂ = 0.321 g O₂/L = 321 mg O₂/L

- 目前广泛应用的测定TOC的方法是燃烧氧化-非色散红外吸收法。
- The currently widely used method for determining TOC is combustion oxidation-non-dispersive infrared absorption method.

其测定原理是：将一定量水样注入高温炉内的石英管，在900-950℃温度下，以铂和三氧化钴或三氧化二铬为催化剂，使有机物燃烧裂解转化为二氧化碳，然后用红外线气体分析仪测定CO₂含量，从而确定水样中碳的含量。因为在高温下，水样中的碳酸盐也分解产生二氧化碳，故上面测得的为水样中的总碳(TC)。

A certain amount of water sample is injected into a quartz tube of the high-temperature furnace. Platinum, cobalt trioxide or chromium trioxide is used as a catalyst to convert the organic matters into carbon dioxide by combustion at the temperature of 900-950℃. Then use the infrared gas analyzer measures the CO₂ content to determine the carbon content of the water sample. Since the carbonate in the water sample can also decomposes to produce carbon dioxide at high temperatures, the total carbon (TC) in the water sample can be measured by the method introduced above.
