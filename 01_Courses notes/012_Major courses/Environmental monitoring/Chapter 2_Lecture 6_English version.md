# Chemical oxygen demand

•  化学需氧量是指在一定条件下，氧化1 L水样中还原性物质所消耗的氧化剂的量，以氧的mg/L表示。

Chemical oxygen demand refers to the amount of oxidant consumed by reducing substances in a 1 L water sample under certain conditions, expressed in mg/L of oxygen.

•  水中的还原性物质：有机物、亚硝酸盐、硫化物、亚铁盐等

Reducing substances in water: organic matter, nitrite, sulfide, ferrous salt, etc.

- Adding a suitable substance to the solution; electrolyzing at a constant intensity and constant current; generating a reagent at the working electrode
- 试剂与被测物质进行定量反应，终点通过电化学方法指示
- The reagent is quantitatively reacted with the test substance, and the end point can be indicated by the electrochemical method
- 依据电解消耗电荷量和法拉第电解定律计算被测物质含量
- According to the amount of charge consumed by electrolysis and Faraday's law of electrolysis, we can calculate the content of the substance to be tested.

# Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)

微生物用于分解水中有机物的氧气量

Amount of oxygen used by microorganisms to decompose organic matters in the water

理论BOD可以通过平衡化学方程来确定，其中所有有机物质都转化为CO₂

Theoretical BOD can be determined by balancing a chemical equation in which all organic matter is converted to CO₂

# Example

计算1.67 x 10⁻³mol葡萄糖（C₆H₁₂O₆）的理论需氧量：

Calculate the theoretical oxygen demand of 1.67 x 10⁻³ moles of glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆):

C₆H₁₂O₆ + O₂ 🡪 CO₂ + H₂O

C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂ 🡪 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O

1.67x 10⁻³mol glucose/L ⨯ 6 mol O₂/ mol glucose ⨯ 32 g O₂/mol O₂ = 0.321 g O₂/L = 321 mg O₂/L

# 1. 五天培养法 (BOD₅法：HJ 505-2009)

# 5-day incubation method

❑定义：也称标准稀释法。其测定原理是水样经稀释后，在20±1℃条件下培养5天，求出培养前后水样中溶解氧含量，二者的差值为BOD₅。如果水样五日生化需氧量超过6 mg/L，则需进行稀释，反之则可直接测定(如较清洁的河水)。

❑Standard dilution method. The water samples are incubated at 20±1°C for 5 days after dilution. Then determine the DO content in the water sample before and after the culture process. The difference between the two value.

❑If the water sample has a BOD₅ value >6 mg/L, it needs to be diluted, otherwise it can be directly measured (such as cleaner river water).

❑对于不含或少含微生物的工业废水，如酸性废水、碱性废水、高温废水或经过氯化处理的废水，在测定BOD₅时应进行接种，以引入能降解废水中有机物的微生物。

❑For industrial wastewater containing no or very little microorganisms, such as acidic wastewater, alkaline wastewater, high-temperature wastewater or chlorinated wastewater, microorganisms should be introduced to degrade organic matters in the wastewater.

# 1. 五天培养法 (BOD₅法：HJ 505-2009) 5-day incubation method

- 当废水中存在着难被一般生活污水中的微生物以正常速度降解的有机物或有剧毒物质时，应将驯化后的微生物引入水样中进行接种；

When the organic substances or highly toxic substances are difficult to be degraded by the microorganisms at a normal speed, the acclimation is needed for microorganisms for inoculation;
- 若水样中含有硝化细菌，应加入适量丙烯基硫脲溶液，抑制硝化反应；

If the water sample contains nitrifying bacteria, an appropriate amount of propylene thiourea solution should be added to inhibit the nitrification reaction;
- 污染的地表水和多数工业废水，含较多有机物，需稀释后再测定，以保证培养过程中有充足的溶解氧；

If the content of organic matters is too high, the water should be diluted before measurement to ensure sufficient dissolved oxygen during the cultivation process;
- 水样稀释程度应使五日培养中所消耗的溶解氧大于2 mg/L, 剩余溶解氧在2 mg/L以上

The dilution of water sample should make the DO consumed in the five-day cultivation >2 mg/L, and the remaining DO >2 mg/L.

# 稀释水 Dilution water

- 稀释水一般用蒸馏水配制 usually prepared by distilled water
- 1) 干净无有机物 Clean and free of organic matters
- 2) (20±1)℃ 曝气 2-8h ， 使水中溶解氧 (DO) 接近饱和 。 (20±1) ℃ aeration for 2-8h, to make DO close to saturation.
- 3) 临用前加入少量氯化钙、氯化铁、硫酸镁等营养盐溶液及磷酸盐缓冲溶液 ， 混匀备用 。 Add a small amount of mixture from nutrient salt solution such as CaCl₂, FeCl₃, MgSO₄, or phosphate buffer solution.
- 4) 稀释水的最佳pH值为7.2，BOD₅应小于0.2mg/L。The optimum pH of the dilution water is 7.2, and the BOD₅ should be less than 0.2 mg/L.

# 电子捕获检测器(ECD) Electronic capture detector (ECD)

- 主要原理为检测室内的放射源放出β-射线(初级电子)，与通过检测室的载气碰撞产生次级电子和正离子，在电场作用下，分别向与自己极性相反的电极运动，形成基流，当具有负电性的组分(即能捕获电子的组分)进入检测室后，捕获了检测室内的电子，变成带负电荷的离子，由于电子被组分捕获，使得检测室基流减少，产生色谱峰信号。
- Main principle: The β- radiation released from sources in detection chamber, collides with the carrier gas passing through the detection chamber to generate secondary electrons and positive ions; Under the action of an electric field, they move towards electrodes with opposite polarity to each other, forming a base current. When a negatively charged enters the detection chamber, it captures the electrons in the detection chamber and becomes negatively charged ions. Due to the capture of electrons by the component, the base current in the detection chamber is reduced, resulting in a chromatographic peak signal.

# 热导检测器(TCD) Thermal conductivity detector (TCD)

• 通用的非破坏性浓度型检测器，理论上可应用于任何组分的检测，但因其灵敏度较低，故一般用于常量分析。A non-destructive concentration detector that can theoretically be applied to the detection of any component. But due to its low sensitivity, it is generally used for constant analysis.

• 基于不同组分与载气有不同的热导率的原理而工作。热导检测器的热敏元件为热丝，如镀金钨丝、铂金丝等。当被测组分与载气一起进入热导池时，由于混合气的热导率与纯载气不同(通常是低于载气的热导率)，热丝传向池壁的热量也发生变化，致使热丝温度发生改变，其电阻也随之改变，进而使电桥输出端产生不平衡电位而作作为信号输出，记录该信号从而得到色谱峰。

• Based on the principle that different components and carrier gases have different thermal conductivity. The thermal sensitive element of the thermal conductivity detector is a hot wire, such as gold-plated tungsten wire, platinum wire, etc. When the tested component and the carrier gas enter the thermal conductivity cell together, the difference in thermal conductivity between the mixed gas and the pure carrier makes the heat from the hot wire to the cell wall change, causing a change in the temperature of the hot wire and its resistance, which in turn generates an unbalanced potential at the output end of the bridge as a signal output. This signal is recorded to obtain the chromatographic peak.

# 火焰光度检测器(FPD) Flame photometric detector (FPD)

• 主要原理为组分在富氢火焰中燃烧时组分不同程度地变为碎片或分子，其外层电子由于互相碰撞而被激发，当电子由激发态返回低能态或基态时，发射出特征波长的光谱，这种特征光谱通过经选择滤光片后被测量。如硫在火焰中产生350-430nm的光谱，磷产生480-600nm的光谱，其中394nm和526nm分别为含硫和含磷化合物的特征波长。

• Main principle: When a component burns in a hydrogen rich flame, it becomes fragments or molecules, and its outer electrons are excited. When the electrons return from the excited state to the low-energy or ground state, they emit a spectrum of characteristic wavelengths. Sulfur produces a spectrum of 350-430nm in flames, while phosphorus produces a spectrum of 480-600nm, with 394nm and 526nm being the characteristic wavelengths of sulfur containing and phosphorus containing compounds, respectively.

• FPD是一种高灵敏度、高选择性的检测器，对含P和S特别敏感，主要用于含P和S的有机化合物和气体硫化物中P和S的微量和痕量分析，如有机磷农药、水质污染中的硫醇、天然气中含硫化物的气体等。Highly sensitive and selective, particularly to P and S, mainly used for trace analysis of P and S in organic compounds and gas sulfides.

# 二、活性污泥性质的测定

# Determination of the properties of activated sludge

- 污泥沉降比 sludge settling velocity (SV)

将混匀的曝气池活性污泥混合液迅速倒进1000 mL量筒中至满刻度，静置30 min，则沉降污泥与所取混合液之体积比为污泥沉降比(%)，又称污泥沉降体积(SV₃₀)，以mL/L表示。

The activated sludge mixture from aeration tank is quickly poured into 1000 mL measuring cylinder until it is full, then the volume ratio of the sludge to the liquid: sludge settling velocity (%) or sludge setting volume (SV₃₀), which is expressed as mL/L.
- 污泥体积指数 Sludge volume index (SVI)

污泥体积指数简称污泥指数(SI)，系指曝气池活性污泥混合液经30 min沉降后, 1g干污泥所占的体积(以mL计)。

The sludge volume index (sludge index for short) refers to the volume (in mL) of 1 g dry sludge after 30 minutes of settling of the activated sludge mixture in the aeration tank.
