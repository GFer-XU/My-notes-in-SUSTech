电感耦合等离子体原子发射光谱法(ICP-OES)

- 电感耦合等离子体焰炬温度可达6000-8000K
- Inductively coupled plasma-optical emission spectrometer
- 当将试样由进样器引入雾化器，并被氩载气带入焰炬时，则试样中组分被原子化、电离、激发，以光的形式发射出能量
- 不同元素的原子在激发或电离时，发射不同波长的特征光谱，故据特征光的波长可进行定性分析；元素的含量不同时，发射特征光的强弱也不同，据此可进行定量分析.
- The temperature of ICP torch can reach 6000-8000K.
- When the sample is introduced into the atomizer from the injector, and brought into the torch by the argon carrier gas, the components in the sample will be atomized, ionized and excited, which emits energy in the form of light.
- Cold atomic absorption spectrometry
- The atoms of different elements emit characteristic spectra with different wavelengths, which enables the qualitative analysis according to the wavelength of the characteristic light.
- When the content of the elements is different, the intensity of the emitted characteristic light is also different, according to which the quantitative analysis can be performed.

原子发射光谱 Atomic emission spectrometry

1）当处于基态的气态原子或离子吸收了一定的外界能量时，其核外电子就从一种能量状态（基态）跃迁到另一能量状态（激发态）；When a gaseous atom or ion in the ground state absorbs a certain amount of external energy, its extranuclear electrons transition from one energy state (ground state) to another energy state (excited state).

2）处于激发态的原子或离子很不稳定，经约10-8秒便跃迁返回到基态，并将激发所吸收的能量以一定的电磁波辐射出来；Atoms or ions in excited states are very unstable, and after about 10-8 seconds, they transition back to the ground state and emit the energy absorbed by the excitation as a certain electromagnetic wave.

3）将这些电磁波按一定波长顺序排列即为原子光谱（线状光谱）；Arranging these electromagnetic waves in a certain wavelength order is called atomic spectrum (linear spectrum).

4）由于原子或离子的能级很多并且不同元素的结构是不同的，因此，对特定元素的原子或离子可产生一系列不同波长的特征光谱，通过识别待测元素的特征谱线存在与否进行定性分析。Due to many energy levels of atoms or ions and different structures of different elements, a series of characteristic spectra with different wavelengths can be generated for specific atoms or ions. Qualitative analysis can be conducted by identifying the presence or absence of characteristic spectral lines of the tested element.

I: Intensity of spectrum

C: Concentration of target element

a: coefficient related to sample composition, speciation and conditions

b: self-absorption coefficient

# （二）冷原子吸收法 Cold (vapor) atomic absorption spectroscopy

# 1. 方法原理 Principle

水样经消解后，将各种形态的汞转变成二价汞，再用氯化亚锡将二价汞还原为单质汞。利用汞易挥发的特点，在室温下通入空气或氮气流将其气化，载入冷原子吸收测汞仪，测量对特征波长(253.7nm)光的吸光度，与汞标准溶液的吸光度进行比较，定量，在一定浓度范围内，吸光度与浓度成正比。

# Workflow

图2.6.3 冷原子吸收汞仪工作流程 of cold atomic fluorescence mercury analyzer

- After digesting the water samples, various forms of mercury will be converted into divalent mercury, which is reduced to elemental mercury by stannous chloride (SnCl₂).
- As mercury is volatile, it will be vaporized by air or nitrogen flow at room temperature, and then will be loaded into a cold atomic absorption mercury analyzer.
- The absorbance of light at a characteristic wavelength (253.7 nm) will be measured, and then concentration will be quantified in comparison with the standard solution.
- In a certain concentration range, the absorbance is proportional to the concentration.

# 氢化物发生-原子吸收法

# 原理

硼氢化钾或硼氢化钠在酸性溶液中，产生氢，将水样中无机砷还原成砷化氢气体，将其用N₂气载入石英管中，以电加热方式使石英管升温至900~1000℃。砷化氢在此温度下被分解形成砷原子蒸汽，对来自砷光源的特征电磁辐射产生吸收，将测得水样中砷的吸光值和标准吸光值进行比较，确定水样中砷的含量。

Potassium borohydride or sodium borohydride generates hydrogen in acidic solutions, reducing inorganic arsenic to arsine gas. It is then loaded into a quartz tube with N₂ gas and heated by electric heating to 900~1000 ℃. Hydrogen arsenide is decomposed into arsenic vapor at this temperature, which absorbs characteristic electromagnetic radiation from an arsenic light source. The measured absorbance value of arsenic in the water sample is compared with the standard absorbance value to determine the arsenic content in the water sample.
