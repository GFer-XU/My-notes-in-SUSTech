# 2.3.1 水样的类型 Types of water samples

# ❑ 瞬时水样 Instantaneous water sample

在某一时间和地点从水体中随机采集的分散单一水样。

Water sample randomly collected from a water body at a certain time and place

- 水质稳定，组分时空变化不大，代表性

stable water quality, consistent in components with time and space, representative
- 变化较大时，隔时、多点采集

When change is large, collect samples at interval time and multisite

# ❑ 混合水样 Composite water sample

- 等时混合水样：某一时段内，同一采样点按等时间间隔采集的等体积瞬时水样混合后的水样：某一时段平均浓度

Composite water sample with the same time interval. At a fixed sampling point, water was collected with equal volume in a time range with the same intervals. It refers to the average concentration in a certain period of time.
- 等比例混合水样：某一时段内，同一采样点所采水样量随时间或流量成比例变化的混合水样，即在不同时间依照流量大小按比例采集的混合水样：流量和污染物浓度不稳定

Proportionally composite water sample: At a fixed sampling point, water sample was collected proportionally to the flow rate at different time within a certain period. It is suitable when the flow rate and pollutants concentration are unstable.

# ①船只采样

适用于一般河流和水库的采样，但不容易固定采样地点，往往数据不具有可比性。

Sampling on boats: suitable for general sampling of rivers and reservoirs, but it is not easy to fix the sampling location, often making the data not comparable.

# ②桥梁采样

安全、可靠、方便，不受天气和洪水的影响，适合于频繁采样，并能在横向和纵向准确控制采样点位置。

Sampling on bridge: safe, reliable and convenient, not affected by weather and floods It is suitable for frequent sampling and can accurately control the sampling point position in both horizontal and vertical directions.

# ③涉水采样

较浅的小河和靠近岸边浅的采样点可涉水采样，但要避免搅动沉积物而使水样受污染。

Sampling by wading into the water: shallow creeks and sampling points near the shore, but should avoid mixing the sediment and contaminating the water sample.

# ④索道采样

在地形复杂、险要，地处偏僻处的小河流，可架索道采样。

Sampling by cableway: Small river located at places with complex and dangerous terrain.

# 2.3.6 流量的测量 Flow Measurement

- 为计算地表水污染负荷是否超过环境容量和评价污染控制效果，掌握废(污)水排放源污染物排放总量和排水总量，采样时需要同步测量废(污)水的流量
- To calculate whether the pollution load for surface water exceeds the environmental capacity and to evaluate the effect of pollution control, know the total amount of pollutants discharged from the wastewater sources and the total amount of drainage, the flow of wastewater should be measured simultaneously.
- 对于较大河流，水利部门都设有水位测量断面，尽可能利用此断面
- For larger rivers, special sections are always set by the government, use the section if possible.
- 若监测河段无水文测量断面，应选择一个水文参数比较稳定、流量有代表性的断面作为测量断面
- If there is no such section, a section with relatively stable hydrological parameters and representative flow rate should be selected as the measurement section.

# 2.3.6 流量的测量 Flow Measurement

# 流量转换器

# 废(污)水流量测量 Wastewater flow measurement

1. # 流量计法 Flow meter method

自由水面的敞开水路用流量计：堰式流量计、水槽流量计

Flow meter for free water surface with open waterway: weir flow meter, water tank flow meter

充满水的管道用流量计：电磁流量计、压差式流量计

Flow meter for pipes filled with water: electromagnetic flowmeter, differential pressure flowmeter
2. # 容积法 Volumetric method

将污水导入已知容积的容器或污水池中，测量流满容器或污水池的时间，然后用其除受纳容器或池的容积，即可求知流量。该方法简单易行，适用于测量污水流量较小的连续或间歇排放的污水。

Introducing sewage into a container with given volume, measuring the time to fill the, and then the flow rate can be calculated. Simple and easy, and is suitable for wastewater continuous or intermittent discharge of sewage with small flow rate.
3. # 溢流堰法 Overflow weir method

适用于不规则的污水沟、污水渠中流量的测量。该方法是用三角形或矩形、梯形堰板拦住水流，形成溢流堰，测量堰板前后水头和水位，计算流量。如果安装液位计，可连续自动测量液位。

Applicable for irregular sewer. Using a triangular or rectangular, trapezoidal raft to stop the water flow and forming an overflow weir, then measuring the water level before and after the raft, and then calculating the flow.

# (2) 调节pH值

# Adjusting the pH

如测定金属离子的水样常用HNO₃酸化至pH为1～2，既可防止重金属离子水解沉淀，又可避免金属被器壁吸附；测定氰化物或挥发性酚的水样加入NaOH调至pH为12时，使之生成稳定的酚盐等。

- For metal ions, acidified to pH 1-2 by HNO₃ to prevent the hydrolysis and precipitation as well as the metal adsorption by the container wall
- For cyanide or volatile phenol, the pH should be adjusted to 12 by adding NaOH to form a stable phenolate.

# (3) 加入氧化剂或还原剂

# Adding oxidant or reducing agent

如测定汞的水样需加入HNO₃(至pH＜1)和K₂Cr₂O₇(0.05%)，使汞保持高价态；测定硫化物的水样，加入抗坏血酸，可以防止被氧化；测定溶解氧的水样则需加入少量硫酸锰和碘化钾固定溶解氧（还原）等。

- For mercury, adding HNO₃ (to pH&#x3C;1) and K₂Cr₂O₇ (0.05%) to maintain high valence of mercury
- For sulfides, adding ascorbic acid added to prevent oxidation
- For DO, adding a small amount of manganese sulfate and potassium iodide should be to fix DO

# 干灰化法

又称干式分解法或高温分解法。其处理过程是：取适量水样于白瓷或石英蒸发皿中，置于水浴上或用红外灯蒸干，移入马弗炉内，于450～550℃灼烧到残渣呈灰白色，使有机物完全分解除去。取出蒸发皿，冷却，用适量2%HNO₃（或HCl）溶解样品灰分，过滤，滤液定容后供测定。

Dry decomposition or high-temperature decomposition. The treatment process is as follows: adding an appropriate amount of water into a white porcelain or quartz evaporating dish, placing it on a water bath or evaporating it with an infrared lamp, transferring it into a muffle furnace, and burning it at 450-550°C until the residue is grayish white, so that the organic matter is completely decomposed and removed. The evaporating dish was taken out, cooled, and the sample ash was dissolved with an appropriate amount of 2% HNO₃ (or HCl), filtered, and the filtrate was made into a volume for measurement.

本方法不适用于处理测定易挥发组分（如砷、汞、镉、硒、锡等）的水样。

This method is not suitable for measuring volatile components such as arsenic, mercury, cadmium, selenium, tin, etc.
