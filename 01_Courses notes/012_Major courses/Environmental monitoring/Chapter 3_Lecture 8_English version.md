# 4.3 一氧化碳的测定

# Determination of carbon monoxide

# 定义 definition：

• CO是一种无色、无味的有毒气体，燃烧时呈淡蓝色火焰。它容易与人体血液中的血红蛋白结合，形成碳氧血红蛋白，使血液输送氧的能力降低，造成缺氧症。

CO is a colorless, odorless, toxic gas that burns with a light blue flame. It easily combines with hemoglobin in human blood to form carboxyhemoglobin, which reduces the ability of blood to deliver oxygen and causes hypoxia.

# 来源 source：

• CO是空气中主要污染物之一，它主要来自石油、煤炭燃烧不充分的产物和汽车排气。

CO is one of the main pollutants in the air, mainly from petroleum, coal-burning products and automobile exhaust.

# 4.4 光化学氧化剂的测定

# Determination of photochemical oxidants

- 总氧化剂是空气中除氧以外的那些显示有氧化性质的物质，一般指能氧化碘化钾析出碘的物质，主要有臭氧、过氧乙酰硝酸酯、氮氧化物等。The total oxidant is substances other than oxygen in the air which exhibit oxidation properties, and are capable of oxidizing potassium iodide to iodine. ozone, peroxyacetyl nitrate, nitrogen oxides, etc.
- 光化学氧化剂是指除氮氧化物以外的能氧化碘化钾的物质，Photochemical oxidants refer to substances other than nitrogen oxide which can oxidize potassium iodide. The relationship between is as below:

ρ(光化学氧化剂) = ρ(总氧化剂)－0.269 ρ(氮氧化物)

ρ(photochemical oxidant) = ρ(total oxidant) - 0.269 ρ(nitrogen oxide)

- 测定空气中光化学氧化剂常用硼酸碘化钾分光光度法。The determination of photochemical oxidants in air is usually carried out by potassium borate potassium iodide spectrophotometry.

# 4.4 光化学氧化剂的测定

# Determination of photochemical oxidants

# ❖ 硼酸-碘化钾分光光度法

# Boric acid-potassium iodide spectrophotometry

# • 原理 principle

用硼酸-碘化钾吸收溶液吸收空气中的臭氧及其它氧化剂，碘离子被氧化析出碘分子的量与臭氧等氧化剂有定量关系,于352nm处测定游离碘的吸光度，与标准色列吸光度比较,可得总氧化剂浓度，扣除NOx参加反应的部分后，即为光化学氧化剂的浓度。

The boric acid-potassium iodide can absorb ozone and other oxidants in the air, and the amount of iodine molecules oxidized and precipitated is quantitatively related to oxidant content. The absorbance of free iodine can be measured at 352 nm. The total oxidant concentration can be obtained, and the concentration of the photochemical oxidant can be obtained after subtracting the portion of the NOx participating in the reaction.

# 4.5 臭氧的测定

# Determination of ozone

# ❖ 概述 Overview

• 臭氧是最强的氧化剂之一，它是空气中的氧在太阳紫外线的照射下或受雷击形成的。臭氧具有强烈的刺激性，在紫外线的作用下，参与烃类和NOₓ的光化学反应.

Ozone is one of the strongest oxidants. It is formed from the oxygen in the air by UV light or lightning. Ozone is highly irritating and can participate in the photochemical reaction of hydrocarbons and NOx under UV light.

• 同时，臭氧又是高空大气的正常组分，能强烈吸收紫外线，保护人和生物免受太阳紫外线的辐射。但是，如O₃超过一定浓度，对人体和某些植物生长会产生一定危害。

Ozone is also a normal component in the high-altitude atmosphere, which can strongly absorb UV rays and protect people and organisms from the radiation of UV light. However, if O₃ exceeds a certain concentration, it will cause certain damage to the growth of human body and certain plants.

# ❖ 测定方法 Methods

• 硼酸-碘化钾分光光度法 Boric acid-potassium iodide spectrophotometry

• 靛蓝二磺酸钠分光光度法：用含有靛蓝二磺酸钠的磷酸盐缓冲溶液作为吸收液采集空气样品 Indigotin disulfonate spectrophotometry: Adsorb air sample with phosphate buffer solution containing indigotin disulfonate.

# 4.6 氟化物的测定

# Determination of fluoride

# ❖ 概念 concept

• 空气中的气态氟化物主要是氟化氢，也可能有少量氟化硅(SiF₄)和氟化碳(CF₄)。含氟粉尘主要是冰晶石(Na₃A1F₆)、萤石(CaF₂)、氟化铝(AlF₃)、氟化钠(NaF)及磷灰石[3Ca₃(PO₄)₂·CaF₂]等。The gaseous fluoride in the air is mainly hydrogen fluoride, and there may be a small amount of silicon fluoride (SiF₄) and carbon fluoride (CF₄). The fluorine-containing dust is mainly cryolite (Na₃A1F₆), fluorite (CaF₂), aluminum fluoride (AlF₃), sodium fluoride (NaF), and apatite [3Ca₃(PO₄)₂·CaF₂].

• 氟化物属高毒类物质，由呼吸道进入人体，会引起黏膜刺激、中毒等症状，并能影响各组织和器官的正常生理功能。对于植物的生长也会产生危害。Fluoride is a highly toxic substance that can enter the human body through the respiratory tract and cause symptoms such as mucous membrane irritation and poisoning, and can affect the normal physiological functions of various tissues and organs. It can also be harmful to the growth of plants.

# ❖ 测定方法 Methods

• 滤膜采样-氟离子选择电极法 Filtration membrane sampling-fluoride ion selective electrode method

• 石灰滤纸采样-氟离子选择电极法 Lime filter paper sampling - fluoride ion selective electrode method

# 4.6 氟化物的测定

# Determination of fluoride

# ❖ 滤膜采样—离子选择电极法

Filtration membrane sampling-ion selective electrode method

用在滤膜夹中装有磷酸氢二钾溶液浸渍的玻璃纤维滤膜或碳酸氢钠-甘油溶液浸渍的玻璃纤维滤膜的采样器采样,则空气中的氟化物被吸收固定，尘态氟化物同时被阻留在滤膜上。采样后的滤膜用水或酸浸取后,用氟离子选择电极法测定。

Use the sampler with the glass fiber membrane impregnated with dipotassium hydrogen phosphate solution or the sodium bicarbonate-glycerol solution for sample collection. The fluoride in the air will be absorbed and fixed, while the dust fluorine will be remained on the membrane. The membrane after sampling is leached with water or acid and then measure by a fluoride ion selective electrode method.

# ❖ 石灰滤纸采样-离子选择电极法

Lime filter paper sampling-ion selective electrode method

用浸渍氢氧化钙溶液的滤纸采样,则空气中氟化物与氢氧化钙反应被固定,用总离子强度调节剂浸取后,用氟离子选择电极法测定。

Collect samples by a filter paper impregnated with the calcium hydroxide solution. The fluoride in the air will be fixed by reacting with calcium hydroxide, and after being leached with a total ionic strength adjuster, it can be measured by a fluoride ion selective electrode method.

# 4.7 硫酸盐化速率的测定

# Overview

污染源排放到空气中的SO₂、H₂S、H₂SO₄蒸气等含硫污染物，经过一系列氧化演变和反应，最终形成危害更大的硫酸雾和硫酸盐雾，这种演变过程的速度称为硫酸盐化速率。

Sulfur-containing pollutants such as SO₂, H₂S, H₂SO₄ vapors emit into the air from the pollution sources, and then they will undergo a series of oxidation evolution and reaction, finally form more harmful sulfuric acid mist and sulfate mist. The speed of this evolution is called sulfation rate.

# Method

- 二氧化铅-重量法 Lead dioxide-gravimetric method
- 碱片-重量法 Alkali tablet - gravimetric method
- 碱片-离子色谱法 Alkali tablet-ion chromatography

---

# 4.7 硫酸盐化速率的测定

# Determination of sulfation rate

# 1. 二氧化铅--重量法

# Lead dioxide - gravimetric method

# ❖ 原理 principle

空气中的SO₂、H₂SO₄蒸气等与二氧化铅反应生成硫酸铅，用碳酸钠溶液处理，使硫酸铅转化为碳酸铅，释放出硫酸根离子，再加入BaCl₂溶液，生成BaSO₄沉淀，用重量法测定。The SO₂, H₂SO₄ vapor in the air can react with lead dioxide to form lead sulfate. The obtained lead sulfate can convert to lead carbonate and release sulfate ion by reacting with sodium carbonate solution. Then the BaCl₂ solution was added to form BaSO₄ precipitate, which can be determined by gravimetric method.

结果以每日在100cm²二氧化铅面积上所含SO₃的毫克数表示。最低检出含量为0.05mgSO₃/(100cm² PbO₂·d)。The results are expressed as: mg of SO₃ on the lead dioxide with an area of 100 cm² per day. The minimum detectable content is 0.05mgSO₃/(100cm² PbO₂·d).

# 4.8 总烃及非甲烷烃的测定

# Determination of total and non-methane hydrocarbons

# ❖ 定义及表示方法 Definition and representation

• 污染环境空气的烃类一般指具有挥发性的碳氢化合物(C₁～C₈)，常用两种方法表示：一种是包括甲烷在内的碳氢化合物，称为总烃(THC)，另一种是除甲烷以外的碳氢化合物，称为非甲烷烃(NMHC)。Hydrocarbons pollutants in the ambient air generally refer to volatile hydrocarbons (C₁～C₈). They are usually represented by two methods: one is the hydrocarbon including methane, called total hydrocarbon (THC), and the other is the hydrocarbon except methane, called non-methane hydrocarbon (NMHC).

# ❖ 来源 source

• 空气中的碳氢化合物主要来自石油炼制、焦化、化工等生产过程中逸散和排放的废气及汽车尾气，局部地区也来自天然气、油田气的逸散。The hydrocarbons in the air mainly come from the exhaust gas emitted from the petroleum refining, coking, chemical and other production processes. In some areas, it comes from the escape of natural gas and oil field gas.

# ❖ 测定方法 Methods

VOCs是指室温下饱和蒸气压超过133.32Pa的有机物，如苯、卤代烃、含氧烃等。VOCs和甲醛是人们关注的室内空气污染的主要有机物，具有毒性和刺激性，有的还有致癌作用。

VOCs and formaldehyde are the main organic pollutants of indoor air pollution, which are toxic and irritating, and some of them have carcinogenic effects.

# 4.9 挥发性有机物(VOCs)和甲醛的测定

# Determination of volatile organic compounds and formaldehyde

# 2. 甲醛的测定

# Formaldehyde

# 1) 酚试剂分光光度法

Phenol reagent spectrophotometry

空气中的甲醛与酚试剂（3-甲基-2-苯并噻唑腙盐酸盐MBTH）反应生成嗪，高铁离子存在下，嗪与酚试剂的氧化产物反应。

The formaldehyde in the air can react with the phenol reagent (3-methyl-2-benzothiazolium hydrochloride MBTH) to form the piperazine. In the presence of ferryl ions, the piperazine will react with the oxidation product of phenol reagent.

# 2) 乙酰丙酮分光光度法

Acetylacetone spectrophotometry

空气中的甲醛被水吸收后，在pH为6的乙酸-乙酸铵缓冲溶液中与乙酰丙酮反应，在沸水浴条件下，迅速生成稳定的黄色化合物。

After the formaldehyde absorbed by water, it will react with the acetylacetone in an acetic acid-ammonium acetate buffer solution with a pH of 6, and rapidly form a stable yellow compound in the boiling water bath.

# 5.5 空气污染指数计算 Air pollution index

2012年改为空气质量指数（Air quality index）

- 空气污染指数(API，现为AQI)是一种向社会公众公布的反映和评价空气质量状况的指标。它将常规监测的几种主要空气污染物浓度经过处理简化为单一的数值形式，分级表示空气质量和污染程度，具有简明、直观和使用方便的优点。
- The Air Pollution Index (API) is an indicator to reflect and evaluate the state of air quality. The major air pollutant concentrations that are routinely monitored are simplified by a single numerical value, which is used to classify the level of air quality and pollution. It is concise, intuitive and convenient.
- 根据我国城市空气污染的特点，以SO₂、NO₂、PM₁₀，CO日平均质量浓度和O₃的8h平均质量浓度，作为计算API的暂定项目，并确定API为50、100、200时，分别对应于我国空气质量标准中日均值的一、二、三级标准的污染浓度限值，500则对应于对人体健康产生明显危害的污染水平。
- According to the urban air pollution in China, the daily average mass concentration of SO₂, NO₂, PM₁₀, CO and the 8 h average mass concentration of O3 are used as tentative projects for calculating API. The API of 50, 100, 200 refer to the concentration of the first, second and third level of the air quality standards respectively. The value of 500 is the pollution level that is obviously harmful to the human health.

# 6.1 采样点的布设 Layout of sampling points

根据我国《大气降水样品的采集与保存》(GB13580.2—92)标准中规定： “Collection and Preservation of Atmospheric Precipitation Samples" (GB13580.2-92)

# (1) 采样点数目 The number of sampling points

Regular monitoring: 3 points for the cities with a population of more than 500,000, and 2 points for the cities with a population of less than 500,000. The layout of sampling points consider the urban and rural areas as well as the clean control points. It should also take care of weather, terrain and landforms.

应尽可能的远离局部污染源，四周无遮挡雨、雪的高大树木或建筑物。The sampling points should be as far away as possible from local pollution sources, and should not be surrounded by tall trees or buildings that can block rain and snow.

温度的测量 temperature

# 测温原理：Temperature measurement principle

- 将两根不同的金属导线连成闭合回路，当两接点处于不同温度环境时，便产生热电势，两接点温差越大，热电势越大。如果热电偶一个接点温度保持恒定(称为自由端)，则热电偶的热电势大小便完全决定于另一个接点的温度(称为工作端)，用毫伏计测出热电偶的热电势，可得知工作端所处的环境温度。
- Two different metal wires are connected into a closed loop. When the two contacts are in different temperature environments, a thermoelectric potential is generated. The greater the temperature difference between the two contacts, the larger the thermoelectric potential.
- If the temperature of a junction of a thermocouple remains constant (called the free end), the thermoelectric potential of the thermocouple is completely determined by the temperature of the other junction (called the working end).
- The thermoelectric potential of the thermocouple can be measured with a millivolt meter, so we can get the ambient temperature at which the working end is located.

压力的测量 Pressure

# 压力的测量 Pressure

- 静压(pₛ)：单位体积气体具有的势能，变现为气体在各个方向上作用于器壁的压力
- Static pressure (pₛ): The potential energy per unit volume of gas, which refers to the pressure of the gas acting on the wall in all directions.
- 动压(pv)：单位体积气体具有的动能，是使气体流动的压力
- Dynamic pressure (pv): The kinetic energy per unit volume of gas, which refers to the pressure that allows the gas to flow.
- 全压(pₜ)：单位体积气体在管道内流动所具有的总能量
- Total pressure (pₜ) : The total energy per unit volume of the gas flowing in the tube.

# （五）烟尘浓度的测定 Smoke dust concentration

# 原理 principle

- 抽取一定体积烟气通过已知重量的捕尘装置
- Extracting a certain volume of flue gas through a dust collector of known weight
- 根据捕尘装置采样前后的重量差和采样体积，计算排气中烟尘浓度。
- Calculate the concentration of smoke dust in the exhaust gas according to the weight difference and sampling volume before and after sampling of the dust collecting device
- 测定排气烟尘浓度必须采用等速采样法，即烟气进入采样嘴的速度应与采样点烟气流速相等。采气流速大于或小于采样点烟气流速都将造成测定误差。
- The determination of exhaust smoke dust concentration must use the constant velocity sampling method. The velocity of the flue gas entering the sampling nozzle should be equal to the velocity of the flue gas at the sampling point. It will cause measurement error when the gas flow velocity is greater or less than the sampling point smoke flow velocity

采样类型 Sampling type
• 移动采样：用一个捕集器在已确定的采样器上移动采样，各点采样时间相同，计算出断面上烟尘的平均浓度

# Blackness of exhaustion

# 概述

烟气黑度是一种用视觉方法监测烟气中排放有害物质情况的指标。The blackness of exhaustion is a visual indicator for monitoring the emission of harmful substances in flue gas.

尽管这一指标难以确定与烟气中有害物质含量之间的精确对应关系，也不能取代污染物排放量和排放浓度的实际监测，但其测定方法简便易行，成本低廉，适合反映燃煤类烟气中有害物质的排放情况。It is difficult to determine the exact relationship between this indicator and the content of harmful substances in flue gas, and it can not replace the actual monitoring of pollutant emission and concentration. But its measuring method is simple and feasible, and its cost is low, and it is suitable for reflecting the emission of harmful substances in coal-fired flue gas.

The principle of the Orsat gas apparatus absorption method

用不同的吸收液分别对烟气中各组分逐一进行吸收，根据吸收前、后烟气体积变化，计算各组分在烟气中所占体积百分数，常用于测定二氧化碳、一氧化碳和氧的含量。Different components of the flue gas are separately absorbed by different absorption liquids, and the volume percentage of each component in the flue gas is calculated according to the volume change of the flue gas before and after the absorption. # 标准气 (Standard Gas)

- 标准气(standard gas)：已知准确浓度的某种或某几种待测物质的空气或惰性气体化合物
- Standard gas: an air or inert gas mixture with known accurate concentrations for an individual gas or several gases to be tested

# 用途
