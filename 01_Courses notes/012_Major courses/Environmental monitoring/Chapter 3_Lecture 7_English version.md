# 2. Harm of air pollution

Air pollution: With the rapid development of industry and transportation, especially the massive use of coal and oil, a large amount of harmful substances will be produced (dust, sulfur dioxide, nitrogen oxides, carbon monoxide, hydrocarbons) and discharged into the air.

When the concentration exceeds the limit concentration allowed by the environment and lasts for a certain period of time, it will change the normal composition of the air, destroy the natural, physical, chemical and ecological balance, and thus endanger people’s life, work and health, and cause damage to natural resources and property, utensils, etc.

空气污染：随着工业及交通运输业等的迅速发展，特别是煤和石油的大量使用，将产生大量有害物质如烟尘、二氧化硫、氮氧化物、一氧化碳、碳氢化合物等排放到空气中，当其浓度超过环境所允许的极限浓度并持续一定时间后，就会改变空气的正常组成，破坏自然的物理、化学和生态平衡体系，从而危害人们的生活、工作和健康，损坏自然资源及财产、器物等。

1. Atmospheric particulate matter
颗粒物的大小决定其沉积于呼吸道中的位置；化学组成决定沉积位置上对组织的影响。

The size of the particles determines the position they are deposited in the respiratory tract; The chemical composition determines the effect on the tissue at the deposition site.
2. Sulfur dioxide
损害肝脏。且由于SO2通常与多种污染物共存，吸入之后产生的复合作用危害更大。

Causes damage to liver. Usually coexists with a variety of contaminants, and the complex will be more harmful.
3. Carbon monoxide
所有大气污染物中散布最广的一种，严重阻碍血液输氧，引起缺氧中毒。

The most widely distributed of all atmospheric pollutants, seriously impeding blood oxygenation, causing hypoxia poisoning.

# 四、空气中的污染物及其存在状态

# 4. Air pollutants and their state

- # （一）分子状态污染物

沸点低，气体分子形式存在，并以分子状态进入大气，或者常温下液体，但挥发性强，受热时易以蒸汽进入大气中，如SO₂，CO， NOx， HCN, 苯，汞，NH₃，H₂S， Pb，Cd，氟化物等。
- # Molecular pollutants

Low boiling point, in the form of gas molecules, and enter the atmosphere as a molecular state, Liquid at room temperature, but highly volatile, easy to steam into the atmosphere when heated, such as SO₂, CO, NOₓ, HCN, benzene, mercury, NH₃, H₂S, Pb, Cd, fluoride, etc.

# 四、空气中的污染物及其存在状态

# 4. Air pollutants and their state

- （二）粒子状态污染物
- ❖  分散在大气中的微小液体和固体颗粒。粒径大小在0.01-100μm之间，复杂的非均相体系。
- ❖  粒径大于100μm的颗粒物能较快地沉降到地面上，称为降尘。
- ❖  粒径小于100μm的颗粒物称为总悬浮颗粒物，是空气常规监测项目，可长期飘浮在空气中，称为可吸入颗粒物或飘尘（IP），又名气溶胶（雾、烟、尘）。
- ❖  颗粒物(PM₁₀)：粒径小于10μm的颗粒物，可通过呼吸进入肺部，在肺泡累积，并通过血液循环输往全身，可吸入颗粒物。
- ❖  颗粒物(PM2.5)：粒径小于2.5μm的颗粒物，粒径更小，活性更强，更易附带有毒有害物质，且在大气中的停留时间长、输送距离远，因而对人体健康和大气环境质量的影响更大。

- Particle state pollutants
- ❖  Dispersed in the atmosphere as tiny liquids and solid particles. particle size (0.01-100μm), complex heterogeneous systems.
- ❖  Particle size >100μm, the particles can settle to the ground, dust.
- ❖  Particle size &#x3C;100μm, total suspended particulate matter a routine air monitoring item.
- ❖  Particulate matter (PM₁₀): &#x3C; 10μm, enter the lungs through breathing, accumulates in the alveoli, and is transported to the body through the blood circulation. Inhalable particles.
- ❖  Particulate matter (PM2.5): &#x3C; 2.5μm.

# 五、空气中污染物的时空分布特点

# 5. Temporal and spatial distribution of pollutants in the air

与其他环境要素中的污染物质相比较，空气中的污染物质具有随时间、空间变化大的特点。

空气污染物的时空分布及其浓度与污染物排放源的分布、排放量及地形、地貌、气象等条件密切相关。

Compared with the pollutants in other environmental mediums, pollutants in the air have a large variation with time and space.

The temporal and spatial distribution of air pollutants and their concentrations are closely related to the distribution of pollutant sources, emissions and topography, geomorphology and meteorology.

# 六、空气中污染物浓度表示方法

# 6. Method for expressing pollutant concentration in the air

# （一）质量浓度

单位体积空气中所含污染物的质量，常用mg/m³或μg/m³表示。对任何状态的污染物都适用。

# （二）体积分数

指单位体积空气中含污染气体或蒸气的体积，常用mL/m³和μL/m³表示。仅适用于气态或蒸气态物质，不受空气温度和压力变化的影响。

两种浓度表示方法之间的换算：

(1) Mass concentration

The mass of pollutants contained in air per unit volume, unit: mg / m³ or μg/m³. Applicable to contaminants in any state.

(2) Volume fraction

The volume of contaminated gas or vapor per unit volume of air, unit: mL/m³ or μL/m³. Only applicable only to gaseous or vaporous substances, not affected by changes in air temperature and pressure. = 22.4 ∙

# 网格布点法

# Grid method

- 有多个污染源，且污染源分布均匀的地区，反应污染物空间分布.
- Multiple pollution sources, equally distributed,
- 主导风向明显，下风向设采样点多，占总数的60%
- Dominant wind direction, 60% of sampling points locate at downwind direction
- 网格大小根据污染源强度、人口分布、人力物力条件等确定
- Grid size is determined by intensity of pollution source, population distribution, human and material conditions

---

# 同心圆布点法

# Concentric circle method

- 多个污染源构成污染群,且大污染源较集中的地区Groups of multiple pollution sources, areas with large pollution sources
- 找出污染群中心,交点作为采样点 The center of the group, intersection point as sampling point
- 不同圆周上采样点数目不一定相等different number of sampling points on different circles
- 常年主导风向的下风向多设一些采样点more sampling points at the downwind direction

---

# 扇形布点法

# Sector method

- 孤立的高架点源,主导风向明显的地区 Isolated elevated point source, area with dominant wind direction
- 以点源作为顶点,主导风向作为轴线 Point source as peak, dominant wind direction as the axis
- 角度一般为45度,可大些,但不能超过90度 The angle is generally as 45q, cannot exceed 90q.
- 每条弧线3-4个采样点 3-4 sample point on each arc
- 上风向设对照点 Control point at upwind direction

# (三) 滤料阻留法 Filter material retention method

- 将滤料（滤纸、滤膜等）放在采样夹上，用抽气装置抽气，则空气中的颗粒物被阻留在滤料上，称量滤料上富集的颗粒物质量，根据采样体积，可算空气中的颗粒物浓度. The maximum absorption wavelength of the material is 577 nm, which can be quantitatively determined by spectrophotometry.

In the presence of glacial acetic acid, nitrous acid is diazotized with p-aminobenzenesulfonic acid, and then coupled with naphthylethylenediamine hydrochloride to form a rose red azo dye. The color depth is proportional to the concentration of NO₂ in the gas sample. Then measure by spectrophotometry.
