# 水质监测的目的

# Purposes of water quality monitoring

# (1) 地表水——经常性监测

# Surface water - regular monitoring

对江、河、水库、湖泊、海洋等地表水和地下水中的污染因子进行经常性的监测，以掌握水质现状及其变化趋势。

"Regular monitoring of pollution factors in surface water and groundwater, such as rivers, lakes, reservoirs, oceans, etc., to grasp the current state of water quality and its trends.

# (2) 生产和生活过程——监视性监测

# Production and life processes - surveillance monitoring

对生产、生活等废（污）水排放源排放的废（污）水进行监视性监测，掌握废（污）水排放量及其污染物浓度和排放总量，评价是否符合排放标准，为污染源管理提供依据。

Surveillance monitoring of wastewater from production and domestic discharge sources, grasp the volume of wastewater discharge and the concentration and total discharge of pollutants, assess compliance with discharge standards, and provide a basis for pollution source management.

# (3) 事故监测——应急监测

# Accident monitoring - emergency monitoring

对水环境污染事故进行应急监测，为分析判断事故原因、危害及制订对策提供依据。

Emergency monitoring of water environmental pollution incidents to provide a basis for analyzing and judging the causes and harms of the incidents and for formulating countermeasures.



---

# 水质监测的目的

# (4) 环境管理——提供数据和资料

为国家政府部门制定水环境保护标准、法规和规划提供有关数据和资料。

Provide relevant data and materials for national government departments to formulate water environmental protection standards, regulations, and plans.

# (5) 环境科学研究——提供数据和资料

为开展水环境质量评价和预测预报及进行环境科学研究提供基础数据和技术手段。

Provide data and methodologies for water environmental quality assessment, and conducting environmental research.

# (6) 环境污染纠纷——仲裁监测

为判断纠纷原因提供科学依据

Provide scientific evidences for judgement.

# Formulation of monitoring program for groundwater quality

# ❖ 采样点布设 Sampling point layout

❑ 地下水一般呈分层流动，进入地下水的污染物、渗滤液等可沿垂直方向运动，也可沿水平方向运动，各层之间会发生串流

Groundwater generally flows in layers, and pollutants, leachate, etc. entering groundwater can move in the vertical direction or in the horizontal direction, and cross-flow occurs between the layers.

❑ 不仅要掌握污染源分布、类型和污染物扩散条件，还要弄清地下水分层和流向

It is necessary not only to grasp the distribution, type and diffusion conditions of pollution sources, but also to clarify groundwater stratification and flow direction.

❑ 两类采样点：背景值监测井、污染控制监测井

Two types of sampling points: background value monitoring well, pollution control monitoring well

❑ 背景值监测井：布设在监测区域未受污染的地段，地下水水流上方，垂直于水流方向

Background value monitoring well: laid in the uncontaminated area of the monitoring area, above the groundwater flow, perpendicular to the direction of the water flow

❑ 污染控制监测井：布设在污染源周围不同位置，特别是下游方向

Pollution control monitoring wells: deployed at different locations around the source of pollution, especially downstream
