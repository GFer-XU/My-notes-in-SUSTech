# (一) 土壤矿物质 Minerals

土壤矿物质是由岩石经风化而来的，一般占土壤固体部分质量的95％～98％。矿物质直接影响土壤性质，又是植物矿质养分的主要来源，故同土壤肥力有密切关系。按成因分为原生和次生矿物质。Soil minerals are derived from weathering of rocks and generally account for 95% to 98% of the mass of soil solids. Minerals affect soil properties directly and are the main source of mineral nutrients for plants. Minerals can be classified as primary and secondary minerals.

# 1. 土壤矿物质的组成 Composition of soil minerals

- (1) 原生矿物质 primary minerals：岩石经过物理风化作用被破碎形成的碎屑，其原来的化学组成没有改变：硅酸盐类、氧化物类、硫化物类、磷酸盐类等。The debris formed by the physical weathering of the rock with unchanged chemical composition: silicates, oxides, sulfides, phosphates, etc.
- (2) 次生矿物质 secondary minerals：原生矿物质经过化学风化后形成的新矿物，其化学组成和晶体结构均有所改变：简单盐类、三氧化物类、次生铝硅酸盐类等。New minerals formed by chemical weathering of the primary minerals. The chemical composition and crystal structure have changed: salts, trioxides, secondary aluminosilicates, etc.

# (二) 土壤有机质

是土壤中有机化合物的总称

Soil organic matter: a general term for organic compounds in soil

- 由进入土壤的植物、动物、微生物残体及施入土壤的有机肥料经分解转化逐渐形成，通常可分为非腐殖物质和腐殖物质两类；是土壤形成的重要基础，与土壤矿物质共同构成土壤的固相部分，占5%。
- Soil organic matter is formed by the decomposition of plants, animals, microbial residues and organic fertilizers applied to the soil. It can be divided into non-humus and humus; it is important for soil formation. The soil organic matter accounts for 5% of the soil mass.
- 土壤有机质中含有大量营养元素，分解后可提供植物生长发育的需要，是植物养分的重要来源。有机质腐解后形成的腐殖质，能把土粒粘结成团粒结构。这种结构保水、保肥能力强，类似储存水肥的小仓库，随时供给植物吸收利用。有机质是微生物的食物，土壤有机质丰富而其他条件又适宜时，就能促进微生物的旺盛活动。
- Soil organic matter contains a lot of nutrients, which can meet the needs of plants growth and development. The humus formed after the decomposition of organic matter can bond the soil particles into agglomerate structure. This structure has strong water retention and fertilizer retention capacity, and provide water and fertilizer for plant absorption and utilization. Organic matter is the food of microorganisms. When the soil is rich in organic matter under suitable conditions, it can promote the vigorous activity of microorganisms.

# (二) 土壤有机质

是土壤中有机化合物的总称

# Soil organic matter

a general term for organic compounds in soil

- # 非腐殖物质

包括糖类化合物 (如淀粉、纤维素等)、含氮有机合物及有机磷和有机硫化合物。10-15%.
- # Non-humus

including saccharides (such as starch, cellulose, etc.), nitrogen-containing organic compounds, and organophosphorus and organic sulfur compounds. 10-15%.
- # 腐殖物质

是植物残体中稳定性较大的木质素及其类似物在微生物作用下，部分被氧化形成的一类特殊的高分子聚合物，具有芳环结构，苯环周围连有多种官能团，如羧基、羟基、甲氧基及氨基等，使之具有表面吸附、离子交换、络合、缓冲、氧化还原作用及生理活性等性能。
- # Humus

A special high molecular polymer formed by oxidation of lignin and its analogues under the action of microorganisms. It has an aromatic ring structure, and there are many functional groups connected to the benzene ring, such as carboxyl, hydroxyl, methoxy and amino groups. So it has properties such as surface adsorption, ion exchange, complexation, buffering, redox and physiological activity.

- Microorganisms (bacteria, fungi, actinomycetes, algae, etc.) and animals (protozoa, earthworms, nematodes, etc.) living in the soil play leading roles in the degradation of organic pollutants and the transformation of inorganic pollutants (such as heavy metals) in the soil. They are the main contributors to the soil purification function.

# (五) 土壤空气 Soil air

- 土壤空气指存在于未被水分占据的土壤孔隙中气体的总称。Soil air refers to the gases present in the pores of soil that are not occupied by water.
- 来源：来源于大气、生物化学反应和化学反应产生的气体(如甲烷、硫化氢、氢气、氮氧化物、二氧化碳等)。Source: Gases are derived from the atmosphere, biochemical reactions, and chemical reactions (such as methane, hydrogen sulfide, hydrogen, nitrogen oxides, carbon dioxide, etc.).
- 影响因素：土壤水分、生物活动、土壤深度、土壤湿度、土壤酸碱度、季节变化等。Influencing factors: soil moisture, biological activity, soil depth, soil pH, seasonal changes, etc.
- 积水的土壤通气不良，导致对植物的危害。Excessive water accumulation in the soil can lead to poor ventilation and cause damage to plants.

# (一) 吸附性 Adsorption

- 与土壤中存在的胶体物质密切相关。Closely related to the presence of colloidal substances in the soil.
- 土壤胶体（具有巨大的比表面积、胶粒带电、分散在水中时产生双电层）
Soil colloid (a large specific surface area, charged colloidal particles, and an electric double layer when dispersed in water)
- - ✓ 无机胶体 Inorganic colloid：粘土矿物、铁铝硅等水合氧化物 hydrated oxides such as clay minerals, iron aluminum silicon
- ✓ 有机胶体 Organic colloid：腐殖质及少量生物活动产生的有机物 humus and organic matter produced by a small amount of biological activity
- ✓ 有机－无机复合胶体 organic-inorganic composite colloid

对有机污染物（如有机磷和有机氯农药）和无机污染物（如Hg⁺、Pb²⁺、Cu²⁺、Cd²⁺等重金属离子）有较强的吸附能力或离子交换吸附能力。
Strong adsorption capacity or ion exchange adsorption capacity for organic pollutants (organic phosphorus and organochlorine pesticides) and inorganic pollutants (heavy metal ions such as Hg⁺、Pb²⁺、Cu²⁺、Cd²⁺).

# (二) 酸碱性 Acidity and alkalinity

土壤的酸碱度可以划分为九级 nine grades.：

- pH&#x3C;4.5：极强酸性土(Extremely strong acid soil)；
- pH=4.5～5.5：强酸性土(Strong acid soil)；
- pH>5.5～6.0：酸性土(Acid soil)；
- pH>6.0～6.5：弱酸性土(Weakly acidic soil)；
- pH>6.5～7.0：中性土(Neutral soil)；
- pH>7.0～7.5：弱碱性土(Weak alkaline soil)；
- pH>7.5～8.5：碱性土(Alkaline soil)；
- pH>8.5～9.5：强碱性土(Strong alkaline soil)；
- pH>9.5：极强碱性土(Extremely alkaline soil)。

# 二、土壤的基本性质 Basic properties

# (二) 酸碱性 Acidity and alkalinity

- 中国土壤的pH大多在4.5～8.5范围内，并呈东南酸西北碱的规律。土壤的酸碱性直接或间接影响污染物在土壤中的迁移转化。
- Soil pH in China is mostly in the range of 4.5-8.5, and is acid in the southeast but alkali in the northwest. Soil acidity and alkalinity can affect the migration and transformation of pollutants in the soil.
- 活性酸度：有效酸度，土壤溶液中游离氢离子浓度
- Active acidity: effective acidity, concentration of the free hydrogen ions in the soil solution
- 潜性酸度：土壤胶体吸附的可交换氢离子和铝离子交换作用后产生的酸度
- Potential acidity: The acidity generated after the ion exchange of exchangeable hydrogen and aluminum ions adsorbed by soil colloids

# 二、土壤的基本性质 Basic properties

# (三) 氧化-还原性 Oxidation-reduction

- 因土壤中含有氧化性和还原性无机物质和有机物质，使其具有氧化性和还原性，可以用氧化还原电位(Eh)来衡量。The soil contains oxidizing and reducing inorganic and organic substances, making it has oxidizing and reducing properties, which can be measured by the oxidation-reduction potential (Eh).
- Eh>300mV：氧化体系起主导作用，土壤处于氧化状态。
- Eh>300mV: The oxidation system plays a leading role and the soil is in an oxidation state.
- Eh&#x3C;300mV：还原体系起主导作用，土壤处于还原状态。
- Eh&#x3C;300mV: The reduction system plays a leading role and the soil is in a reduction state.

# 三、土壤背景值 Soil Background value

- 判断土壤是否受到污染或污染程度的标准。Criteria for the evaluation of soil contamination
- 土壤背景值又称土壤本底值，它代表一定环境单元中的一个统计量的特征值。The soil background value means the eigenvalue of a statistic in a certain environmental unit.
- 背景值（地质学）：指在各区域正常地质地理条件和地球化学条件下元素在各类自然体中的正常含量。Background value (geology): refers to the normal content of elements in natural bodies under normal geological and geographical conditions and geochemical conditions in each region.
- 环境科学上：指在未受或少受人类影响的情况下，尚未受或少受污染和破坏的土壤中元素的含量。In environmental science: refers to the content of elements in soil that has not been or is less contaminated and destroyed.

# 他国土壤安全经验

# 美国：“超级基金”法案

拉夫运河位于美国东北部，20世纪40年代被废弃成工业废弃物垃圾填埋场，随着，化学废弃物渗入土壤。居住在周边地区的居民随之出现各种怪病。经调查，在土壤中发现11种致癌物。拉夫运河的污染物清理工作直到2004年才宣告完成，用时24年，耗资4亿多美元。

1980年，美国通过法案，批准设立污染场地管理与修复基金，即“超级基金”法案，只要认定污染，就可以无限期对污染企业进行追责，并被征收高额罚金。

（20世纪90年代开始，美国用于污染土壤修复方面的投资已有几千亿美元，但至今仍有50万幅地块需要治理，美国超级基金在投资污染治理方面，存在投入产出比不高的情况，中国应当避免。）

# 四、采样点的布设 Sampling point

# (一) 布设原则 Principles

1. 合理地划分采样单元 Divide the sampling unit reasonably.
2. 对于土壤污染监测，哪里有污染就在哪里布点。For monitoring of soil pollution, layout the points where there is pollution.
3. 采样点不能设在田边、沟边、路边、肥堆边及水土流失严重或表层土被破坏处。Sampling points should not locate at fields, ditches, roadsides, the edge of the compost and places where soil erosion is serious or topsoil is destroyed.

# (二) 采样点数量 Number of sampling points

- 根据监测目的、区域范围大小及其环境状况等因素确定。
- According to the purpose of monitoring, the size of the area and the environmental conditions.
- 一般每个采样单元最少设3个采样点。
- Generally, there are at least 3 sampling points per sampling unit.
- 单个采样单元内采样点数可按下式估算 The number of sampling points in a single sampling unit can be estimated as below:

n = ( s                 t )2 / d

式中：

- n——每个采样单元布设的最少采样点数；
Minimum number of samples per sample unit
- s——样本相对标准偏差，即变异系数；
Relative standard deviation of sample, coefficient of variation
- t——置信因子，当置信水平为95%时，t值为1.96；
Confidence factor, when the confidence level is 95%, the t is 1.96
- d——允许偏差，当规定抽样精度不低于80%时，d取0.2。
Allowable deviation, when the required sampling accuracy is not less than 80%, d is 0.2.

# (三) 采样点布设方法 Sampling point layout method

- # 1. 对角线布点法 Diagonal Layout Point

适用于面积较小、地势平坦的污水灌溉或污染河水灌溉的地形端正的田块。由田块的进水口向对角引一直线，将对角线划分为若干等分(一般3-5等分)，在每等分的中点处采样。Applicable to regular and small-scale flat field irrigated with sewage or polluted river water. Draw a diagonal line from the water inlet, and divide the line into several equal parts and samples are taken at the midpoint of each aliquot.
- # 2. 梅花形布点法 Plum Blossom-shaped Layout Point

适用于面积较小、地势平坦、土壤物质和污染程度较均匀的地块。中心点设在两对角线相交处，一般设5-10个采样点。Suitable for flat and small area, which is equally polluted. The center point is located at the intersection of two diagonal lines, generally 5-10 sampling points.
- # 3. 棋盘式布点法 Checkerboard shaped Layout Point

适用于中等面积、地势平坦、地形完整开阔的地块，一般设10个以上分点。该法也适用于受固体废物污染的土壤，应设20个以上分点。Applicable to medium area, flat and open terrain, generally with more than 10 points. Also applicable to soil contaminated by solid waste, and more than 20 points.

---


# 4. 蛇形布点法 Serpentine Layout Point

适用于面积较大、地势不很平坦、土壤不够均匀的田块。布设采样点数目较多。For large area, uneven terrain and uneven soil, always with many sampling points.

# 5. 放射状布点法 Radial Layout Point

适用于大气污染型土壤。以大气污染源为中心，向周围画射线，在射线上布设采样点。在主导风向的下风适当增加采样点之间的距离和采样点数量。For air-polluted soil. Air pollution source as the center, draw the radiation, and lay sampling points on the radiation. Increase the distance between sampling points and the number of sampling points in the downwind of the dominant wind direction.

# 6. 网格布点法 Grid Layout Point

适用于地形平缓的地块。将地块划分成若干均匀网状方格，采样点设在两条直线的交点处或方格的中心。农用化学物质污染型土壤、土壤背景值调查常用这种方法. For relatively flat area, divide the area into uniform mesh squares, and the sampling points are located at the intersection of two straight lines or at the center of the squares. Commonly used in investigating background values of soil and soil polluted by agricultural chemicals.

对于综合污染型土壤，还可以采用两种以上布点方法相结合的方法。For comprehensive polluted soils, a combination of two or more methods can be used.

# 样品的代表性和采样误差的控制

- 土壤的不均一性是造成采样误差的最主要原因。
- Soil heterogeneity is the main cause of sampling errors.
- 土壤是固、气、液三相组成的分散体系，各种外来物进入土壤后流动、迁移、混合较难，所以采集的样品往往具有局限性。一般情况下，采样误差要比分析误差高得多。为保证样品的代表性，必须采取以下两个技术措施控制采样误差。
- Soil is a dispersed system consisting of solid, gas and liquid phases. It is difficult for the substances to flow, migrate and mix after they enter the soil, so the collected samples often have limitations. Generally speaking, the sampling error is much higher than the analysis error. To ensure the representativeness of samples, the following two technical measures must be taken to control sampling errors.



---



# 1. 采样前的准备工作

（1）采样前要进行现场勘察和有关资料的收集，根据土壤类型、肥力等级和地形等因素将研究范围划分为若干个采样单元，每个采样单元的土壤要尽可能均匀一致。Before sampling, field investigation and data collection should be carried out. According to soil type, fertility grade and topography, the research area should be divided into several sampling units. The soil of each sampling unit should be as uniform as possible.

（2）要保证有足够多的采样点，使之能充分代表采样单元的土壤特性。采样点的多少，取决于研究范围的大小，研究对象的复杂程度和试验研究所要求的精密度等因素。采样点设置过少，所采样品的偶然性增加，缺乏足够的代表性；采样点设置过多，则增大了采样的工作量，浪费了人力、物力和财力。Ensure that there are enough sampling points to fully represent the soil characteristics of the sampling unit. The number of sampling points depends on the size of the research scope, the complexity of the research object and the precision required by the experimental research. If the number of sampling points is too small, the contingency of the sampled products will increase and the representation will be insufficient. If the sampling points are too many, the workload of sampling will be increased and the human, material and financial resources will be wasted.

• 测定要点：称取通过1 mm孔径筛的土样10 g于烧杯中，加无二氧化碳蒸馏水25 mL，轻轻摇动后用电磁搅拌器搅拌1 min，使水和土充分混合均匀，放置30 min，用pH计测量上部浑浊液的pH值。Weigh 10 g of soil sample that can pass through 1-mm sieve into beaker, then add 25 mL distilled water without carbon dioxide, shake and stir for 1 minute with electromagnetic stirrer, so that water and soil are fully mixed, stand for 30 minutes. • 土粒的粗细及水、土比例均对pH值有影响。一般酸性土壤的水土比（质量比）保持5:1～1:1；碱性土壤水土比以1:1或2.5:1为宜，水土比增加，测得pH值偏高。The size of soil particles and the ratio of water to soil have an effect on the pH value. The water-soil ratio (mass ratio) of acidic soil is 5:1-1:1: the ratio of water to soil in the alkaline soil is preferably 1:1 or 2.5:1. When the water-soil ratio increases, the pH value will be higher.

• 风干土壤和潮湿土壤测得的pH有差异，尤其是石灰性土壤，由于风干作用，使土壤中大量二氧化碳损失，导致pH偏高，因此风干土壤的pH为相对值。The pH values of air-dried soil are different from those of wet soil, especially for calcareous soil. Because of air-drying, a large amount of carbon dioxide is lost in the soil, which leads to high pH. Therefore, the pH of air-dried soil is a relative value.

# (一) 六六六和滴滴涕 C₆H₆Cl₆ and DDT

六六六和滴滴涕属于高毒性、高生物活性的有机氯农药，在土壤中残留时间长，其半衰期为2-4年。C₆H₆Cl₆ and DDT belong to organochlorine pesticides with high toxicity and biological activity. They remain in soil for a long time and their half-life is 2-4 years.

# ⮚危害Harmfulness：

土壤被六六六和滴滴涕污染后，对土壤生物会产生直接毒害，并通过生物积累和食物链进入人体，危害人体健康。Soil contaminated by C₆H₆Cl₆ and DDT will cause direct toxicity to soil organisms and enter the human body through biological accumulation and food chain, endangering human health.

# ⮚测定方法Determination method：

广泛使用气相色谱法。其最低检出限质量分数为0.05~4.87ug/kg。Gas chromatography is widely used. The minimum detection limit mass fraction was 0.05-0.87 ug/kg.

# 1. 方法原理 principle

用丙酮-石油醚提取土壤样品中的六六六和滴滴涕，经硫酸净化处理后，用带电子捕获检测器的气相色谱仪测定。根据色谱峰进行两种物质异构体的定性分析；根据峰高(或峰面积)进行各组分的定量分析。

C₆H₆Cl₆ and DDT in soil samples were extracted with acetone-petroleum ether and purified with sulfuric acid, then determined by gas chromatography with electron capture detector. Qualitative analysis of isomers of two substances was carried out according to chromatographic peaks, and quantitative analysis of each component was carried out according to peak height (or peak area).

带电子捕获检测器的气相色谱仪；全玻璃进样器；长1.8～2.0m，内径2～3mm螺旋状硬质玻璃填充柱。

Gas chromatography with electron capture detector; full glass injector; spiral hard glass packed column with a length of 1.8 to 2.0 m and an inner diameter of 2 to 3 mm.
