# History

- Around 10,000 BC, humans began to live in communities, resulting in mass production of solid waste.
- In 500 BC, a law was passed to require all waste material to be deposited more than a mile out of town. Because the piles of rubbish next to the city walls provided an opportunity for invaders to scale up and over the walls.
- In 14 AD, Rome developed a waste collection program.
- In 1840s, The “Great Sanitary Awakening” was spearheaded by a lawyer, Edwin Chadwick, who argued that there was a connection between disease and filth.
- In late 1800s, recycling was by individuals who scoured the streets and the trash piles looking for material of value. The first organized municipal recycling program was attempted in 1874 in Baltimore.

---

# History In USA

- In 1657, the residents of New York forbade throwing garbage into streets.
- In 1866, the Metropolitan Board of New York declared war on trash, forbidding the throwing of garbage or dead animals into streets.
- In 1887, the first incinerator in the New World was built.
- In 1895, the garbage problem finally became a factor in politics. Municipal collection systems were created. Carry the waste by barge into the bight area and dump it into the water.
- In 1934, the fouling of beaches forced the passage of federal legislation, making the dumping of municipal refuse into the sea illegal.
- In 1935, the first hole-in-the-ground that was periodically covered with dirt, a precursor of today’s modern landfill was started in California.
- In 1959, the American Society of Civil Engineers published the first engineering guide to sanitary landfilling.

# Love Canal

was a major environmental and public health disaster in the United States during the 1970s. It involved a neighborhood in Niagara Falls, New York, built on a former chemical waste dump.

# Key points:

- History: In the 1940s–1950s, Hooker Chemical Company dumped over 21,000 tons of toxic industrial waste into the canal, which was later covered and sold.
- Development: The land was sold to the local school board, and homes and a school were built on the site in the 1950s–1970s.
- Health crisis: By the late 1970s, residents reported foul odors, chemical seepage, and health issues like birth defects, miscarriages, and cancers.
- Activism: Led by local resident Lois Gibbs, community protests and media attention forced government action.
- Response: In 1978, New York declared a state of emergency, relocated families, and the site was added to the Superfund National Priorities List in 1983.
- Legacy: Love Canal became a symbol of corporate negligence and spurred the creation of the U.S. Superfund law (1980) to clean up hazardous waste sites. The case highlighted the long-term dangers of improper chemical disposal and the importance of community advocacy in environmental justice.

# 二、危险废物的定义和鉴别

# 定义（ Definition ）

指在国家危险废物名录中，和根据国务院环境保护部门规定的危险废物鉴别标准认定的具有危险性的废物。(contained in the National Hazardous Wastes List and identified as hazardous based on the standard of the Environmental Protection Departments)

工业废物中的危险废物占总量的5%-10%(Hazardous wastes account for 5%-10% of the industrial wastes)

# 鉴别（ identification ）

1. 是否引起或严重导致人类和动植物死亡率增加 (Cause serious increases in human, animal and plant mortality?)
2. 是否引起各种疾病的增加 (Cause an increase in various diseases?)
3. 是否降低对疾病的抵抗力 (Reduce the resistance to diseases?)
4. 是否在处理、贮存、运送、处置或其他管理不当时，对人体健康或环境会造成现实的或潜在的危害 (Pose a real or potential hazard to human health or the environment during the handling, storage, transportation, disposal ?)

---

# 危险废物的特性 (Characteristics of hazardous waste)

- • 含闪点低于60℃的液体 (Liquid with a flash point below 60℃)
- • 经摩擦或吸湿和自发的变化具有着火倾向的固体 (Solid that has a tendency to ignite through friction or moisture absorption and spontaneous changes)
- • 含水固体废物，或本身不含水但加入水后其浸出液的pH≤2或pH≥12.5的废物 (Wet solid waste, or dry waste which can generate leachate with pH ≤ 2 or pH ≥ 12.5 after adding water)
- • 或最低温度为55.7℃以下时对钢制品每年的腐蚀深度大于0.64cm/a 的废物 (Wastes with a corrosion depth > 0.64 cm/a for steel products at a minimum temperature of ≤ 55.7℃)
- • 无爆震时就很易发生剧烈变化 (dramatic change without strong knock);
- • 和水剧烈反应 (significant reaction with water);
- • 能和水形成爆炸性混合物 (can form explosive mixture with water);
- • 和水混合产生毒性气体、蒸汽或烟雾 (can generate toxic gases, vapors or fumes when mixed with water);
- • 有引发源或加热时能爆震或爆炸 (can explode when activated or heated);
- • 常温常压下易爆炸和爆炸性反应 (explosive at normal temperature and pressure);
- • 根据其它法规定义的爆炸品 (explosive materials mentioned in other regulations).

# Sanitary landfills

Sanitary Landfills are designed to greatly reduce or eliminate the risks that waste disposal may pose to the public health and environmental quality.

- The bottom and sides of landfills are lined with layers of clay or plastic to keep the liquid waste, known as leachate, from escaping into the soil.
- The leachate is collected and pumped to the surface for treatment. Monitoring wells are dug in the vicinity of the landfill to monitor groundwater quality.
- Some sanitary landfills are used to recover energy. The natural anaerobic decomposition of the waste in the landfill produces landfill gases which include carbon dioxide, methane and traces of other gases.

# ❖  (四) 份样量 (Sample mass)

份样量是指构成一个份样的固体废物的质量。样品多才具有代表性，因此份样量不能少于某一限度。份样量达到一定限度后，再增加质量不能显著提高采样的准确度 (the mass of solid waste for a sample. To be representative, the sample mass cannot be less than a certain limit. When the sample mass reaches a certain limit, the accuracy of the sampling cannot be increased significantly.)

取决于固体废物的粒度 (Depend on the particle size of the solid waste)

最小份样量大致与固体废物的最大粒径的a次方成正比，与固体废物的不均匀度成正比 (The minimum sample mass is roughly proportional to the α power of the maximum particle size of the solid waste, and is also proportional to the heterogeneity of the solid waste.)

# 二、样品的制备(Samples preparation)

# (三)制样程序(Sample preparation program)

1. 粉碎(Crush)

把全部样品逐级破碎，过5mm筛孔。粉碎过程中，不可随意丢弃难于破碎的粗粒 (All samples were crushed step by step and then passed through a 5mm mesh. Coarse particles which are difficult to break should not be discarded.)
2. 缩分(shrink)

将样品于清洁、平整、不吸水的板面上堆成圆锥型，每铲物料自圆锥顶端落下，使均匀地沿锥尖散落，不可使圆锥中心错位。反复转堆，至少三周，使其充分混合。然后将圆锥顶端轻轻压平，摊开物料后，用十字板自上压下，分成四等份，取两个对角的等份，重复操作数次，直至不少于1kg试样为止 (The sample is stacked into a conical shape, and the materials should fall from the top of the pile. Repeat the stack for at least three times. Then gently flatten the top of the cone, spread the material, press it from the top with a cross plate, divide it into four equal parts, take two diagonal parts, repeat the operation several times until the final sample is no less than 1kg)

# 三、样品水分的测定 (Determination of sample moisture)

1. 无机物(inorganic)：称取样品20g左右于105℃下干燥，恒重至±0.1g，测定水分含量

Weigh 20g of sample and dry at 105°C, then determine the moisture content
2. 有机物(organic)：样品于60 ℃下干燥24h，测定水分含量

The sample should be dried at 60 °C for 24h for moisture content
3. 固体废物测定结果以干样品计算，当污染物含量小于0.1％时以mg/kg表示，含量大于0.1％时则以百分含量表示，并说明是水溶性或总量

The measurement results are calculated based on dry weight. When the pollutant content is less than 0.1%, it should be expressed as mg/kg. When the content is larger than 0.1%, it should expressed as percentage. Specify whether it is water soluble or total amount.

# (Method for monitoring harmful characteristics)

# 一、急性毒性的初筛试验

# (Screening test for acute toxicity)

# 毒性试验动物

(Animals for toxicity test)：体重18-24g小白鼠 (或200-300g大白鼠) Mouse weight of 18-24g or 200-300g

# 试验步骤

(Test procedure)：

1. 称取制备好的样品100g，置于500mL具磨口玻璃塞的三角瓶中，加入100mL水 (pH为5.8~6.3) (固液比为1:1)，振摇3min于室温下静止浸泡24 h，用中速定量滤纸过滤，滤液留待灌胃用
2. Weigh 100g of the prepared sample, place it in a 500mL triangular flask with a ground-in glass stopper, add 100mL water (pH 5.8~6.3) (solid-liquid ratio is 1:1), shake for 3min and soak at room temperature for 24 h, filtered with medium-speed filter paper, and the filtrate is reserved for intragastric administration.
3. 对10只小白鼠（或大白鼠）进行一次性灌胃，每只灌浸出液0.50(或4.80)mL，对灌胃后的小白鼠（或大白鼠）进行中毒症状观察，记录48 h内动物死亡数
4. Intragastric infusion for 10 small mice (or big mice) with 0.50 (or 4.80) mL filtrate. Then observe the poisoning symptoms and record the number of animal deaths within 48 hours.

# 二、易燃性的试验方法(Test method for flammability)

易燃性 (Flammability)：测定闪点 (Determine the flash point)

- 闪点较低的液态状废物和燃烧剧烈而持续的非液态状废物，由于摩擦、吸湿、点燃等自发的化学变化会发热、着火，或可能由于它的燃烧引起人体或环境的危害 (Liquid waste with low flash point and solid waste that burns violently and continuously, may cause heat or fire due to spontaneous chemical changes such as friction, moisture absorption, ignition, etc., and therefore may cause damage to human or environment.)
- 采用闭口闪点测定仪测定闪点。温度计采用1号温度计(-30-170℃)或2号温度计(100-300℃) 。防护屏采用镀锌铁皮制成，高度550-650mm，宽度以适用为度，屏身内壁漆成黑色 (Use a closed flash point tester to determine the flash point)

# 试验步骤(Test procedure)

- 按标准要求加热试样至一定温度，停止搅拌，每升高1℃点火一次，至试样上方刚出现蓝色火焰时，立即读出温度计上的温度值 (Heat the sample to a certain temperature according to the standard requirements, stop stirring, and ignite every elevated 1°C. When the blue flame appears above the sample, read the temperature value on the thermometer immediately.)
- 操作过程细节参见国标GB/T261-2008 (闪点测定法 宾斯基-马丁闭口杯法）(The operation process details should be referred to the national standard GB/T261-2008.)

- The other is to determine the corrosion rate of steel products below 55.7℃。

# 三、腐蚀性的试验方法(Test method for corrosive)

# pH值的测定 (pH计或酸度计) (pH value by pH meter or acidity meter)

- 对稠状物料可离心或过滤后，测其液体的pH值
- After centrifuging or filtering the thick material, measure the pH value of the liquid
- 对粉、粒、块状物料，称取制备好的样品50g(干基)，置于1 L塑料瓶中，加入新鲜蒸馏水250 mL，使固液质量比为1:5，加盖密封后，放在振荡机上，于室温下，连续振荡30 min，静置30 min后，测上清液pH值，每种废物取两个平行样品测定其pH值，差值不得大于0.15。否则应再取样品重复试验，取中位数报告结果
- For powder, granules and bulk materials, weigh 50g sample (dry), place it in a 1 L plastic bottle, add 250 mL of fresh distilled water, and make the solid-liquid mass ratio as 1:5. Shake it continuously for 30 min at room temperature. After standing for 30 min, measure the pH of the supernatant. Take two parallel samples for each waste and measure the pH value. The difference should not exceed 0.15. Otherwise, the sample should be repeated again.
- 对含水量高、呈流态状的稀泥或浆状物料，可将电极直接插入进行pH值测量
- For mud or slurry materials with high water content, the pH value can be measured by directly inserting the electrode.

# 五、遇水反应性的试验方法(Test method for water reactivity)

# 遇水反应性 (Water reactivity)：

1. 固体废物与水发生剧烈反应而放出热量，使体系温度升高。可用温升试验测定 (The solid wastes react with water violently and release heat, which cause temperature increase.)
2. 与水反应释放出有害气体，如乙炔、硫化氢、砷化氢、氰化氢 (React with water and release harmful gas, such as acetylene, hydrogen sulfide, arsine, hydrogen cyanide).

# 试验步骤 (Test procedures)：

称取固体废物50g，置于250mL的反应器内，加入25 mL水 (用1mol/L HCl调节pH值为4)，加盖密封后，固定在振荡器上，振荡频率为110±10次/min，振荡30 min后停机，静置10 min. 用注射器抽气50 mL，注入不同的5 mL吸收液中，测定其氰化氢、硫化氢、砷化氢、乙炔的含量。

Weigh 50g solid waste, place it in a 250 mL reactor, add 25mL water (pH 4 by 1mol/L HCl), seal it, and fix it on the shaker at a frequency of 110±10 times/min, after 30 min of shaking, stop and stand for 10min. Pump 50mL with a syringe, inject into different 5mL of absorption liquid, and determine the content of hydrogen cyanide, hydrogen sulfide, arsine and acetylene).

# 二、生活垃圾特性分析

# 2. 垃圾的粒度分级

粒度采用筛分法，按筛目排列，依次连续摇动 15 min，依次转到下号筛子，然后计算每一粒度微粒所占的百分比。如果需要在试样干燥后再称量，则需在70℃的温度下烘干24 h，然后再在干燥器中冷却后筛分。

The particle size is sieved, arranged according to the mesh, continuously shaken for 15 minutes, sequentially transferred to the next sieve, and then the percentage of particles per particle size is calculated. If it is necessary to weigh the sample after drying, it should be dried at 70 ° C for 24 h, then cooled in a desiccator and sieved。

# 二、生活垃圾特性分析

# 3. 淀粉的测定 (Starch test)

堆肥过程中，需借助淀粉量分析堆肥的腐熟程度，生成淀粉碘化络合物，颜色变化取决于堆肥的腐熟程度.

During the composting process, the maturity degree of the compost is analyzed by the amount of starch. A starch iodinated complex can be generated and the color change depends on the degree of composting.

# 分析试验的步骤(Analytical test steps):

1. 将1g堆肥置于100mL烧杯中，滴入几滴酒精使其湿润，再加20mL 36％的高氯酸 (Place 1g of compost in a 100mL beaker, add a few drops of alcohol to make it moist, and add 20mL of 36% perchloric acid);
2. 用纹网滤纸(90号纸)过滤 (Filter with mesh filter paper);
3. 加入20mL碘反应剂到滤液中并搅动 (Add 20 mL of iodine reagent to the filtrate and stir);
4. 将几滴滤液滴到白色板上，观察其颜色变化。深蓝-浅蓝-灰-绿-黄 (Drop a few drops of the filter onto a white plate and observe the color change. Dark blue – light blue - gray-green–yellow).

# 4. 生物降解性的测定 (biodegradability)

垃圾中含有大量天然的和人工合成的有机物，有的容易被生物降解，有的难以降解，可以在室温下对垃圾生物降解适当估计COD (Municipal solid waste contains plenty of natural and synthetic organic matter, some of which are easily biodegraded while some are difficult to be degraded. COD can be properly estimated for biodegradation of waste at room temperature.)

# 在室温下粗测COD的试验步骤 (Procedures for COD measurement at room temperature):

1. 称取0.5 g已烘干磨碎的试样于500 mL锥形瓶中 (Weigh 0.5 g of dried sample in a 500mL conical flask);
2. 准确量取20mL[c1/6(K₂Cr₂O₇)＝2mo1/L]重铬酸钾溶液加入样品瓶中并充分混合 (Add 20 mL[c1/6(K₂Cr₂O₇)＝2mo1/L] of potassium dichromate solution into the sample);
3. 量取20mL硫酸加到样品瓶中 (Measure 20 mL of sulfuric acid into the sample);
4. 在室温下将这一混合物放置12 h且不断摇动 (Shake this mixture at room temperature for 12 h);
5. 加入约15 mL蒸馏水 (Add about 15 mL of distilled water);
6. 再依次加入10 mL磷酸，0.2 g氟化钠和30滴指示剂 (Then add 10 mL of phosphoric acid, 0.2 g of sodium fluoride and 30 drops of indicator);
7. 用标准硫酸亚铁铵溶液滴定 (Titration with standard ammonium ferrous sulfate solution);
8. 用同样的方法在不放试样的情况下做空白试验 (Blank test).

# 5．热值的测定：热量计法或热耗法

(Calorific value test: calorimeter method or heat loss method)

焚烧是有机工业固体废物、生活垃圾、部分医院垃圾处置的重要方法，部分垃圾焚烧可以发电
(Incineration is an important method for disposing organic industrial waste, municipal solid waste and some hospital waste. Waste incineration can generate electricity)

热值是垃圾焚烧处置的重要指标，分为高热值(H₀)和低热值(Hₙ)
(Calorific value is an important indicator for waste incineration disposal, which is divided into high calorific value (H₀) and low calorific value (Hₙ))

垃圾中可燃物质燃烧时产生的反应水一般以水蒸气形式挥发，因此，相当一部分能量不能被利用。
所以当垃圾高热值测出后，应扣除水蒸气和燃烧时加热物质所需要的热量，由高热值换算成低热值
(The water generated during combustion is generally volatilized as water vapor, and therefore consumes a considerable portion of energy. Therefore, after measuring the high calorific value of the waste, the energy required for the steam and heating the substances during combustion should be deducted, which can be converted to a low calorific value)

# 高热值与低热值换算：

| Hₙ | = | Hₒ | × | 100 - (w + W) / 100 - 1 | - 5.85W |
| -- | - | -- | - | ----------------------- | ------- |

式中：

- Hₙ——低热值，kJ/kg；
- Hₒ——高热值，kJ/kg；
- w₁——惰性物质含量（质量分数）, ％；
- W——垃圾的表面湿度，％；
- WL——剩余的和吸湿性的湿度，％。

# 三、渗滤液分析 (Leachate analysis)

填埋初期，由于地下水和地表水的流入、雨水的渗入以及垃圾本身的分解，产生大量污水，称为垃圾渗滤液 (In the early stage of landfill, a large amount of sewage is produced due to the inflow of groundwater and surface water, the infiltration of rainwater, and the decomposition of the waste itself, which is called landfill leachate).

是填埋处置中最主要的污染源 (The most important pollution source of landfill disposal)

---

# 三、渗滤液分析 (Leachate analysis)

# 1．渗滤液的特性 (Leachate characteristics)

1. 成分的不稳定性：主要取决于垃圾组成 (Instability: mainly depends on the composition of the waste)
2. 浓度的可变性：主要取决于填埋时间 (The variability of concentration: mainly depends on the landfill time)
3. 组成的特殊性：垃圾中存在的物质，渗滤液中不一定存在。而一般废水中存在的组分渗沥液中也不一定存在，例如油类、氰化物、铬和汞等 (The particularity of the composition: the substances present in the municipal may not exist in the leachate. The components in wastewater may be also not contained in leachate, like oils, cyanides, chromium and mercury)
4. 渗滤液是不同于生活污水的特殊污水 (Leachate is a special sewage different from domestic sewage)

Color, total solids, total dissolved solids and total suspended solids, sulfate, ammonia nitrogen, Kjeldahl nitrogen, chloride, total phosphorus, pH, BOD, COD, potassium, sodium, total number of bacteria, total coliforms, etc. The total number of bacteria and E.coli are unique monitoring items in China。

# 4.5 有害物质的毒理学研究方法

# (Methods for toxicology of hazardous substances)

环境是复杂体系，污染物种类繁多，含量多变，各污染因素之间存在拮抗、相加、相乘等作用 The environment is complicated with a wide variety of pollutants in various contents. There are antagonistic, additive, and multiplying effects among various pollution factors.

环境综合质量很难以各污染物个别影响来评价 It is difficult to evaluate the comprehensive quality of the environment by the individual impact of each pollutant.

利用生物在该环境中的反应，确定环境的综合质量，是理想和重要的手段 It is an ideal and important means to determine the overall quality of the environment by using the response of the organisms in the environment.

环境毒理学研究方法：通过用实验动物对污染物进行毒性试验，确定污染物毒性和剂量的关系，找出毒性作用和阈剂量(阈浓度) (Environmental toxicology: Determine the toxicity and threshold dose (threshold concentration) by testing the toxicity of pollutants to experimental animals.

---


# 一、实验动物的选择及毒性试验分类

# (Selection of experimental animals and classification of toxicity tests)

# （一）实验动物的选择(Selection of experimental animals)

- ❖常用的动物有小鼠、大鼠、兔、豚鼠、猫、狗和猴等 (Commonly used animals: mice, rats, rabbits, guinea pigs, cats, dogs and monkeys)
- ❖鱼类有鲢鱼、草鱼和金鱼等 (salmon, grass carp and goldfish)
- ❖不同动物对毒性反应不一致 (Different animals have inconsistent toxicity)
- ❖要判断某种物质的环境最高允许浓度，除了毒性外，还要考试感官性状、稳定性、及自净过程等因素 (To determine the maximum allowable concentration of a substance in the environment, it is also necessary to examine the sensory traits, stability, and self-purification process besides toxicity)
- ❖根据实验动物的毒性试验所得到的毒性大小、安全浓度和半数致死浓度等数据不能直接推断到人体，还要进行流行病学调查研究才能反映人体受影响情况 (Data such as toxicity, safe concentration and median lethal concentration obtained from the toxicity test of experimental animals cannot be directly inferred to the human body, and epidemiological investigations are required to reflect the situation for human beings)。



---


# （二）毒性试验分类(classification of toxicity tests)

- # ❖急性毒性试验 (Acute toxicity test)

一次（或几次）投给实验动物较大剂量的化合物，观察在短期内（一般24小时到2周以内）中毒反应 (A large dose of the compound was administered to the experimental animals once (or several times) and the toxic reaction was observed in a short period of time (typically within 24 hours to 2 weeks))
- # ❖亚急性毒性试验 (Subacute toxicity test)

一般用半致死剂量的1/5~1/20，每天投毒，连续半个月到3个月左右。了解毒物是否有积累作用和耐受性 (Generally, 1/5~1/20 of the semi-lethal dose is administered daily, and last for half a month to 3 months. Whether the poison can be accumulated or the tolerance can be generated)
- # ❖慢性毒性试验(Chronic toxicity test)

用较低剂量进行3个月到1年的投毒，观察病理、生理、生化反应以及寻找中毒诊断指标，并为制定最大允许浓度提供科学依据 (Use a low dose continuously for 3 months to 1 year. Observe pathological, physiological, biochemical reactions and find diagnostic indicators for poisoning, and provide a scientific basis for the development of the maximum allowable concentration)。
- # ❖终生性毒性试验 (Lifetime toxicity test)
