# 7.1.1 声音和噪声 Sound and noise

# 一、声音的定义 Definition of sound

声音的本质是波动。受作用的空气发生振动，当振动频率在20～20000Hz时，作用于人的耳鼓膜而产生的感觉称为声音。 Sound is wave motion. When the frequency of air vibration is within the range of 20-20000z, the feeling produced by the human eardrum is called sound.

# 二、噪声及其主要危害 Noise and its main hazards

噪声：人们生活和工作所不需要的声音叫噪声。 Noise: The sound which is not needed in people's life and work is called noise.

主要危害：损伤听力、干扰人们的睡眠和工作，诱发疾病，干扰语言交流，强噪声还会影响设备正常运转和损环建筑结构。 The main influence: damage to hearing, interfere with people's sleep and work, diseases, interfere with language communication. Strong noise will also affect the normal operation of the equipment and damage to the building structure.

# 7.1.2 声音的物理特性和量度

# Physical properties and measurement of sound

# 一、声音的发生、频率、波长和声速

# Generation, frequency, wavelength and speed of sound

# 1. 声音的发生 generation

当物体在空气中振动，使周围空气发生疏、密交替变化并向外传递，且这种振动频率在20～20000Hz之间，人耳可以感觉，称为可听声，简称声音。When the object vibrates in the air, the surrounding air is sparsely and densely changed and transmitted outwards. When the vibration frequency is between 20 and 20,000 Hz, human can feel it, which is called audible sound, referred to as sound.

次声——频率低于20Hz infrasound: frequency below 20Hz

超声——频率高于20000Hz ultrasound: frequency higher than 20000Hz

# 2. 频率 frequency

声源在一秒钟内振动的次数叫频率，记作f，单位为Hz。 The number of times the sound source vibrates in one second is called the frequency, which is denoted as f, and the unit is Hz.

# 二、声功率、声强和声压

# Sound Power, Sound Intensity and Sound Pressure

# 1.声功率 (W) Sound Power

声功率是指单位时间内，声波通过垂直于传播方向某指定面积的声能量。在噪声监测中，声功率是指声源总声功率，单位为W。Sound power refers to the sound energy of the sound wave passing through a specified area perpendicular to the direction of propagation per unit time. In noise monitoring, the sound power refers to the total sound power of the sound source, and the unit is W.

# 2.声强 (I) Sound Intensity

声强是指单位时间内，声波通过垂直于声波传播方向单位面积的声能量，单位为W/m²。 Sound intensity refers to the sound energy of per unit area when sound waves pass through the area which is perpendicular to the direction of sound wave propagation. The unit is W/m².

# 3.声压 (p) Sound Pressure

声压是由于声波的存在而引起的压力增值。声波是空气分子有指向、有节律的运动。声压单位为Pa。Sound pressure is a pressure increase due to the presence of sound waves. Sound waves are directional, regular movements of air molecules. The unit is Pa.

# 1. 分贝decibel

分贝是指两个相同的物理量(如A₁和A₀) 之比取以10为底的对数并乘以10(或20)。

Decibel refers to the division of two identical physical quantities (such as A1 and A0) and then take the base 10 logarithms, and then is multiplied by 10 (or 20).

分贝符号为“dB”，它是无量纲的，是噪声测量中很重要的参量。上式中A₀是基准量(或参考量)，A₁是被量度量。被量度量和基准量之比取对数，该对数值称为被量度量的“级”，亦即用对数标度时，所得到的是比值，它代表被量度量比基准量高出多少“级”。

Take "dB“ to denote decibel. A dimensionless and important parameter for noise. A₀ is a reference quantity, and A₁ is a measured quantity. This value is called the “level” of the measured quantity, which means the “levels” of the measured quantity higher than the reference quantity.

# 五、噪声的物理量和主观听觉的关系

# 1、响度和响度级

# 1). 响度

响度是人耳判别声音由轻到响的强度等级概念，响度的单位叫“宋”（sone），1宋的定义为声压级为40dB，频率为1000Hz，且来自听者正前方的平面波形的强度。如果另一个声音听起来比这个大n倍，则声音的响度为n宋。

Loudness is the concept of the intensity level that the human ears discriminate the sound from soft to loud. The unit of loudness is called "sone", and the 1 song is defined as the strength of a plane waveform whose sound pressure level is 40dB, the frequency is 1000Hz, and is produced right in front of the listener. If another sound sounds is n times larger than this, the loudness of the sound is n sone.

# 2). 响度级

定义1000Hz纯音声压级的分贝值为响度级的数值，任何其他频率的声音，当调节1000Hz纯音的强度使之与这声音一样响时，则这1000Hz纯音的声压级分贝值就定为这一声音的响度级值。响度级的单位叫“方”（phon）。

Define the decibel value of the 1000Hz pure sound pressure level as the loudness level. For sound with other frequencies, we adjust the intensity of the 1000Hz pure sound to make it sound similarly, then the decibel value of the sound pressure level for this 1000Hz pure sound is determined as the loudness level of the measured sound. The unit of the loudness level is called "phon".

# 2、计权声级 Weighted sound level

实际上声源所发生的声音几乎都包含很广的频率范围，为了能用仪器直接反应人的主观响度感觉的评价量，有关人员在噪声测量仪器——声级计中设计了一种特殊的滤波器，叫做计权网络，通过计权网络测得的声压级，并不再是可观的物理量声压级，而叫做计权声压级，简称声级。

Actually, the sounds generated by the sources always contain a wide range of frequencies. To directly respond the evaluation of the subjective loudness feeling of people by the instrument, people designed a special wave filter for the noise measuring instrument, which is called the weighting network. The sound pressure level measured by the weighting network is no longer a considerable physical sound pressure level, but is called as the weighted sound pressure level, referred to as the sound level.

# 7.1.3 噪声测量仪器

# 一、声级计 Sound level meter

# 1. 声级计的工作原理 Principle

声级计，又叫噪声计，是一种按照一定的频率计权和时间计权测量声音的声压级和声级的仪器，是声学测量中最常用的基本仪器，是一种主观性的电子仪器。

光污染的物理量 Physical quantity of light pollution

能否看清一个物体，或能否辨别物体上的细微部分，都与物理表面的被照明程度有关。为了标明物体被照明程度，引进了照度的物理量。

Whether an object can be seen clearly, or whether the fine parts of the object can be distinguished, depends on the degree of illumination on the physical surface. In order to indicate the illumination degree of the object, the physical quantity of illumination is introduced.

照度：反映光强度的一种单位，其物理意义是照射到单位面积上的光通量，单位是勒(克斯 lux)，表示单位面积的光通量，流明(lm)为光通量的单位。

Illumination  A quantity that reflects the intensity of light. Its physical meaning is the luminous flux per unit area. The unit is Lux, which represents the luminous flux per unit area. Lumen (lm) is the unit of luminous flux.
